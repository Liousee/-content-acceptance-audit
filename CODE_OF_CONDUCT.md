# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent; critique ideas, not people.
- Respect privacy: never post another person's data, screenshots, account details, or
  campaign information.
- Follow the safety rules in `docs/safety.md`; do not pressure maintainers to weaken them.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Encouraging CAPTCHA bypassing, unauthorized account access, or platform abuse.
- Including real client/creator data or credentials in issues or pull requests.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block contributors who violate this code.
Report concerns privately via the security contact described in `SECURITY.md`.

This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
